# Design context

Keel is the creator's DevOps internship portfolio project, aimed at interviewers and
engineers investigating synthetic payment reliability. The creator wants the frontend
to feel like a payment is moving, with unmistakable feedback while operations run.

Maintain the established restrained navy/green identity and bundled Plex font.
Motion should feel calm, tangible and trustworthy: show movement only for real active
states, never imply successful settlement, fabricate backend progress, or delay results
for a decorative animation. Work on ordinary desktop and mobile browsers without new
runtime dependencies. Respect reduced motion and keep keyboard interaction immediate.
